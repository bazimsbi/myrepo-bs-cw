How this guide is organized

The SOW's six topic areas collapse into three focus areas based on Jay's grouping: Market Insights & Corporate Metrics (Groups A, B), Business Development (Group C), and Strategy (Groups D, E, F). Each focus area gets one tailored question set below — the same set works for every session within that area, since Jay grouped by topic rather than by distinct sub-focus.

Every session also opens with a short baseline set (Section 2) asked identically across all six groups. This exists specifically to probe three data sources the SOW implies but no prior discovery call has named: revenue cycle/claims adjudication (charges, allowed amounts, denials), workforce/HR (FTEs, RVUs, turnover), and patient experience/survey data. Asking the same way in every session makes the answers comparable and lets us map ownership across the org.

Section 2 · Every Session

Baseline questions — ask identically in all six

Keep these first regardless of group, before the tailored questions. Comparable answers across groups are the point — they'll show which teams already touch each of the three newly-identified source gaps, and how consistently "market" and "service line" are defined.

B1What reports or dashboards do you use today for your team's core questions, and in what tool (Tableau, Power BI, Excel, other)?
B2Where does the underlying data come from — do you know the source system, or does someone else assemble it for you?
B3Do you use or need charges, allowed amounts, or denials data? If yes, what system is it from today?
B4Do you use or need FTE, staffing, or RVU data? If yes, what system is it from today?
B5Do you use or need patient experience or Likelihood-to-Recommend data? If yes, what system is it from today?
B6Which demographic cuts matter most to you — ZIP, payer, sex, age, race, ethnicity — and is anything missing today?
B7What's the biggest data-quality or trust issue with your current reporting?
