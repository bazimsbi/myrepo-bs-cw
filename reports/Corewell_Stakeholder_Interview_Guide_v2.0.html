How this guide is organized

Every session opens with a baseline block (Section 2) built from the 12 requirements the tracker scopes to all groups — volume, revenue/denials, workforce, access/capacity, quality-financial linkage, drill-path ownership, demographic governance, and the executive scorecard. Asking it identically in all six sessions keeps answers comparable and surfaces who actually owns each definition today.

Each group then gets a focus-specific block built only from the requirements the tracker scopes to that group: Market Insights & Corporate Metrics (Groups A, B — market/volume/growth), Business Development (Group C — cost, margin, opportunity evaluation), and Strategy (Groups D, E, F — the full requirement set, since Strategy is scoped to every topic area). Every question below is tagged with the REQ ID it validates, so answers can be logged straight into the tracker's Interview_Capture sheet.

What changed since v1.0
Resolved
Epic lands via Caboodle, not Clarity — the tracker's working principles are explicit: "select Caboodle domains from approved requirements." Closes the open question from the architecture review.
Source named
Workforce source is now named: Workday / HR / Finance. Closes the "workforce source unnamed" gap flagged in the Fact & Dimension Model v0.2 gap register.
Constraint confirmed
"Do not create patient-level joins between Epic and MHA/Trilliant" is now a stated working principle, not just a flagged risk — the payer/provider and MHA/Epic identity-linkage gaps are design rules, not open questions.
Still open
Revenue-cycle/claims source remains TBD (REQ-002/003 note "payer/claims source TBD"). Patient experience, quality, capacity and equipment sources remain grouped under "Additional Sources" — still unnamed.

Section 2 · Every Session

Baseline questions — ask identically in all six

Built from the 12 tracker requirements scoped to "All Groups." Each validates ownership, current-state grain, source system, and governance — the fields the tracker's Interview_Capture sheet expects back.

B1
By care setting — encounter, visit, procedure, discharge, patient-day — which volume definition is authoritative today, and over what period/benchmark do you compare?REQ-001
B2
For revenue, charges and denials, which report or system is your source of truth today, and who owns that definition?REQ-002 · REQ-003
B3
Where do FTE, staffing ratio, RVU and turnover figures come from today — Workday, Strata, Epic, or a manually reconciled spreadsheet?REQ-007
B4
Which access/capacity measures do you actually use (third-next-available, OR block time, bed/room utilization), and what threshold signals a real constraint vs. normal variation?REQ-011 · REQ-012 · REQ-013
B5
How do you connect quality/outcome measures — readmissions, LOS, mortality, safety, patient experience — to financial or operational performance today? Is that link manual?REQ-014 · REQ-015
B6
When you drill from a system-wide number down to a specific procedure or diagnosis, what's the actual path, and who owns the hierarchy/mapping you use?REQ-016
B7
For ZIP/payer/sex/age/race/ethnicity cuts, do you ever need patient-level detail, or is aggregated-cohort level always sufficient? Any minimum-cell-size or suppression rule you already follow?REQ-017
B8
Which handful of KPIs would you consider "must-have" on a system-wide executive scorecard, and what currently produces them?REQ-018
