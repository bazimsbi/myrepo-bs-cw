Executive summary

The model centers on a single conformed grain — the patient encounter — with satellite facts for procedure-level detail, cost accounting, revenue cycle, workforce, market volume, access/throughput, and patient experience, matching the SOW's six topic areas. Twelve conformed dimensions are shared across every fact, built directly from the SOW's explicit attribute list (§4.5) so the model reflects what the business asked for, not just what the two reviewed sources happen to contain.

Four facts are buildable today from MHA and Trilliant alone: Encounter, Procedure Line, the Diagnosis bridge, and Market Volume. Five are designed but blocked on sources not yet reviewed — Cost (Strata), Claim/Revenue, Workforce, Access/Throughput, and Patient Experience — three of which (Claim/Revenue, Workforce, Patient Experience) were identified only after reading the SOW; no prior discovery call named them.

Every dimension attribute below is traced to its source field where one exists, or marked pending where it isn't yet available — this is a mapping document, not an aspiration.

Section 5 · Bridges

Multi-valued relationships need bridge tables

MHA carries up to 29 secondary diagnoses and up to 30 procedure codes as columns on a single discharge row — a fan-out pattern that breaks a simple star schema. Both need a bridge (factless fact) rather than a direct dimension key on Encounter.

BRG_ENCOUNTER_DIAGNOSIS Available — MHA + Trilliant
Grain: one row per (encounter, diagnosis, sequence)
Purpose
Un-pivots MHA's 29 secondary-diagnosis columns and Trilliant's visit_all_diagnosis_codes into rows, with a sequence number and a primary/secondary flag.
Sources
MHA: PRINCIPALDIAGNOSIS, SECONDARYDIAGNOSIS1–29. Trilliant: visit_primary_diagnosis_code, visit_all_diagnosis_codes.
BRG_ENCOUNTER_PROCEDURE Available — MHA + Trilliant
Grain: one row per (encounter, procedure, sequence)
Purpose
Un-pivots MHA's 29 (IP, ICD-10-PCS) or 30 (OP, CPT/HCPCS-shaped) procedure columns into rows. Feeds FACT_PROCEDURE_LINE at Trilliant's native grain.
Sources
MHA: PRINCIPALICDPROCEDURE, SECONDARYICDPROCEDURE1–29 (IP); OUTPATIENTPROCEDURE1–30 (OP). Trilliant: procedure_code (native line grain, no un-pivot needed).

Section 6 · Open Gaps

What this model surfaces that wasn't visible before

Blocks 3 facts

Revenue Cycle / claims-adjudication source not yet named

Charges, allowed amounts, denials, and charge capture (SOW: Encounter Volume & Revenue) have no identified source. Likely Epic Resolute or a clearinghouse — confirm in a stakeholder session.

Blocks 1 fact

Workforce / HR source not yet named

FTEs, staffing ratios, turnover, RVUs (SOW: Workforce & Productivity) require a system — Workday, Kronos, or similar — not mentioned in any discovery call to date.

Blocks 1 fact

Patient Experience / survey source not yet named

Likelihood to Recommend and patient-experience metrics (SOW: Quality, Safety, Experience) require a platform such as Press Ganey or NRC Health.

Blocks DIM_MARKET

Geography grain conflict — ZIP5 (MHA) vs. ZIP3 (Trilliant)

DIM_MARKET_GEOGRAPHY must either degrade MHA to ZIP3 or maintain parallel geography hierarchies. Business decision, not a technical one — see the interview guide's Q8.

Blocks DIM_SERVICE_LINE

No canonical service-line taxonomy exists yet

Trilliant provides service_line directly; MHA has no equivalent field and would need derivation from MSDRG/MSMDC or procedure category. The two will disagree until one taxonomy is built and both sources are mapped into it.

Affects FACT_ENCOUNTER

Person-level join between MHA and Trilliant remains impossible

Carried forward from v0.1 — MHA has no patient identifier and Trilliant's person_id is a Trilliant-internal token. FACT_ENCOUNTER conforms on NPI, geography, date, and code sets, never on patient identity, across these two sources.
